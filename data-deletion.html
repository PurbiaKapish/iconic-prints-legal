User Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Iconic Prints respects your privacy. You may request deletion of eligible personal information associated with your interactions with our WhatsApp-based customer support and photo printing service.

How to Request Data Deletion

To request deletion of your data, please send an email to:

Email: iconicbunny16@gmail.com

Please include:

  • Your name
  • Your WhatsApp phone number used to contact Iconic Prints
  • Your Meesho Order ID, if applicable
  • A clear statement that you want your personal data deleted

What May Be Deleted

Depending on the request and applicable requirements, this may include:

  • Customer photos submitted for printing
  • WhatsApp messages stored in our order-processing system
  • Order screenshots
  • Customer contact information stored by our internal system
  • Other eligible information associated with the customer request

Processing Your Request

We will review the request and delete eligible information from systems under our control within a reasonable period.

Some information may be retained where necessary for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, security, or legitimate business purposes.

Third-Party Platforms

Deleting information from Iconic Prints' internal systems does not automatically delete information held independently by third-party platforms such as WhatsApp, Meta, or Meesho.

Customers may need to contact those platforms separately regarding information controlled by them.
