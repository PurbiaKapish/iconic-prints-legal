Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Iconic Prints ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when customers interact with our WhatsApp-based order support and photo printing services.

1. Information We May Collect

When you contact Iconic Prints, we may receive information such as:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number
  • Your WhatsApp profile name
  • Messages you send to us
  • Meesho Order ID or related order information
  • Photos and images you voluntarily send for printing
  • Order screenshots sent for verification
  • Basic technical information required to process WhatsApp messages

2. How We Use Your Information

We use this information only for purposes related to our services, including:

  • Identifying and verifying customer orders
  • Receiving and processing photos for customized printing
  • Counting and organizing submitted photos
  • Providing order-related support through WhatsApp
  • Matching submitted Order IDs with Meesho order information
  • Managing printing and order-processing workflows
  • Detecting duplicate, missing, or incorrect order information
  • Improving the reliability and security of our service

3. Customer Photos

Photos are provided voluntarily by customers for customized printing. We process photos only for fulfilling and supporting the related order.

We do not use customer photos for advertising, resale, or unrelated purposes. Photos are retained only as reasonably necessary for order processing, customer support, dispute resolution, and operational requirements.

4. WhatsApp and Meta

Our service may use the WhatsApp Business Platform provided by Meta. Messages and media sent through WhatsApp may also be processed by Meta according to Meta's own privacy policies and terms.

5. Meesho Order Information

Customers may provide Meesho Order IDs or screenshots so that we can match photo submissions with the correct order. We may also use seller order information exported from Meesho for internal reconciliation and fulfillment.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell customer personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary for operating our service, such as WhatsApp/Meta, hosting, storage, or order fulfillment systems.

We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect our business, customers, or legal rights.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect customer information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

Personal information and customer photos are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to process orders, provide support, maintain business records, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations.

9. Data Deletion Requests

Customers may request deletion of eligible personal information by contacting Iconic Prints using the email address below.

Some information may need to be retained where required for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or legitimate business purposes.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for customers making legitimate product orders. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for unrelated purposes.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technology, or legal requirements change. The latest version will always be available on this page.
